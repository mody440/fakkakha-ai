# Fakkakha AI — 90-Second Judge Demo Script

## 0–10s — Hook

> “Most AI tutors prove that the model knows the answer. Fakkakha is designed to prove that the **student** learned.”

Open the app and tap **Demo Mode**.

## 10–30s — Ask a question

Use a middle-school question such as `3x + 4 = 19`.

Point out that the system first identifies the topic, then moves into a guided session instead of immediately dumping a solution.

Say:

> “The student has to expose their reasoning. That gives the AI evidence about the misconception, not just the final answer.”

## 30–50s — Mistake Detective

Open Mistake Detective with an intentionally flawed attempt.

Show:
- where the first mistake occurred;
- why it is wrong;
- the tiny explanation;
- the targeted practice prompt.

Say:

> “This is the key difference: Fakkakha stores patterns of misunderstanding, so future practice can target the weakness.”

## 50–70s — Student model

Open Progress and Mistake Patterns.

Point to topic-level mastery and repeated mistake categories.

Say:

> “The student is not just chatting with an AI. They are building a lightweight learning profile that changes after meaningful evidence.”

## 70–90s — Measurement

Open Exam Mode.

Say:

> “Finally, Exam Mode turns the conversation into an assessment checkpoint. The answer key stays server-side, scoring happens on the backend, and the result feeds back into the learning model.”

Close with:

> “Our north-star metric is not prettier answers. It is independent performance and transfer after the AI is gone.”
