# Fakkakha AI — Competition Pitch

## One-liner

**Fakkakha AI turns AI from an answer machine into a learning coach that diagnoses misconceptions, guides reasoning, and measures mastery.**

## The problem

Generative AI can make homework dramatically easier while making learning harder to observe. A student can copy a correct solution without understanding the concept, and a conventional chatbot has little reason to notice.

## The insight

The most valuable signal is not the final answer. It is **how the student reasons on the way to the answer**.

## The product

Fakkakha creates a closed learning loop:

1. Understand the question.
2. Identify subject, topic and intent.
3. Guide the student through the next reasoning step.
4. Diagnose mistakes at the point where reasoning diverges.
5. Update topic-level mastery and mistake patterns.
6. Verify progress with targeted assessment.

## Why it is different

- **Socratic by default:** the student participates instead of receiving a finished solution.
- **Mistake memory:** repeated misconception categories become actionable signals.
- **Evidence loop:** Exam Mode provides an explicit checkpoint for independent performance.
- **Privacy-aware architecture:** authenticated ownership, RLS, server-side model keys and bounded inputs.
- **Low-friction adoption:** anonymous onboarding first, optional account continuity later.

## What we will prove

We will evaluate Fakkakha against an answer-first baseline using pre/post learning gain, transfer, misconception repair, independent solution rate, time-to-mastery, factuality, safety and cost.

**We will not publish performance claims before running the benchmark.**

## Long-term vision

A personal AI learning layer that can travel across subjects, devices and curricula while remaining accountable to measurable student growth.
